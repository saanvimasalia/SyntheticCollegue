Sources: metrics_log.txt, gaps_20261012_162613.csv

Why this looks human

Across 150 sampled action delays the coefficient of variation is CV=2.51 (mean 1.85s, median 0.19s, p90 5.15s, max 29.48s). A metronomic/scripted agent sits near CV=0; the heavy right tail here is the human 'thinking pause' signature.

On the telemetry side, 5 Sysmon process-event gaps give CV=0.68 (mean 154.58s, max 277.24s). Clean runs log only a handful of process events, so this series is sparse by design — the per-action histogram above is the fuller picture of the same timing model.

Excluded 50 action-call markers (launch×1, press×19, scroll×22, type_text×6, wait_for_window×2) — these are event timestamps with no duration, not sampled delays, so they're left out of the histograms above.
